I build AI projects that bring together **models, data, and applications**. My work spans visual inspection, depth and 3D pose estimation, knowledge graphs for open data, and AI-powered learning experiences.

I enjoy building the full path: preparing data, integrating models, evaluating their behavior, and turning them into applications people can explore.

## How I work

- **Traceable evidence.** Preserve sources, configurations, and documentation so results can be understood and checked.
- **Evaluation in context.** Distinguish benchmarks, simulations, and hardware experiments, documenting their conditions and limits.
- **Reproducibility.** Use pinned environments, scripts, and tests to make experiments easier to repeat.
- **Practical integration.** Connect models with cameras, APIs, interfaces, and tools for AI agents.
